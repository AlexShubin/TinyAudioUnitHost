# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 18 May 2026

Tiny Audio Unit Host ("the app") is designed to run entirely on your Mac. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no network communication. It does not contact any server operated by us or any third party.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores audio routing preferences, the currently selected Audio Unit, and saved presets on your Mac, inside its macOS sandbox container at `~/Library/Containers/com.alexshubin.TinyAudioUnitHost/`. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app or its container removes all of it.

## Microphone access

The app requests permission to use your microphone or audio interface input. Audio is processed in real time through the Audio Unit you choose and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted.

## Audio Unit plugins

The app loads third-party Audio Unit plugins that you have already installed on your Mac. Those plugins are owned and operated independently and have their own privacy and licensing terms. We do not transmit information about which plugins you use.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it complies with COPPA and similar regulations by default.
